FLEET OPERATIONS · MOVING GROUND STATION · PX4 × 3

Three drones, one moving carrier.

Three PX4 instances launch in turn from pads on a carrier vehicle, fly separate inspection legs at 3, 4 and 5 m, then land back on their pads while the carrier drives. A ground station grants one launch and one landing at a time; every drone keeps its own C++ supervisor and PX4 failsafes.

Trails are PX4 estimates converted to world coordinates; grey dashed routes are each drone's plan. After touchdown a drone is drawn on its pad, because PX4's own estimate stops following a moving deck once it has landed (explained in the SITL guide); the deck camera shows where the drones physically are. Drones are drawn 1.5× for visibility. This is a recorded replay, not a live console.

Launches, phases and landings

Real: three PX4 flight-firmware instances with EKF2, Gazebo physics for the drones and the carrier, ROS 2 over XRCE-DDS, and each drone's C++ supervisor. The carrier's road is obstacle-free by design, and the carrier's motion comes from Gazebo's velocity controller. Recording two cameras runs this simulation below real time; every adapter judges freshness on simulation time, as PX4 does.
