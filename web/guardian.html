GUARDIAN DRONES · SENSE · WARN · KEEP CLEAR · NON-KINETIC

Guardians that see first, and never close in.

Drones on watch posts around a station extend its sensors, give the crew and the command center time to decide, keep themselves clear of intruders, and survive jamming and GNSS spoofing. Decisions are split by time budget: the drone acts alone within seconds, the station within its delegated authority, and the center decides everything beyond protection.

Who may decide what

Non-kinetic by design: the guardians sense, report, warn, keep clear, shelter and fall back; nothing steers toward a threat, and the tests enforce it. Any response to the threat itself belongs to the center and to authorised systems outside this lab. The fast simulator is kinematic with illustrative round-number parameters, not the specification of any real sensor or aircraft. In the PX4 flights the threats, birds, sensors, jamming and GNSS spoofing are simulated; the guardians' flight software, supervisors and PX4 are real.
